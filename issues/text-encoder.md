# T5-XXL: most of the checkpoint, little of the clock

**Status:** open  
**Labels:** memory, quantization, text-encoder  
**Basis:** arithmetic ceilings only  
**Device:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090

The text encoder is 9.53 GB of the 10.85 GB kept on the
card, but only 2.0% of the time at 20 steps. It runs once; the DiT runs twenty
times.

| steps | t5-encode | dit-step | vae-decode |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 20 | 2.0% | 94.3% | 3.7% |
| 4 | 8.1% | 76.7% | 15.1% |

So the work here is **memory**, which the frontier scores:

- **Quantization.** fp8 takes it to 4.76 GB and NVFP4 to
  2.68 GB, freeing up to 6.8
  GB of a 31 GiB card.
- **Caching.** The output depends only on the prompt, so an unchanged prompt need not be encoded
  again. That matters most at few steps.
